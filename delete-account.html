Delete your account and data

Applies to Nivee's citizenship-test study apps (Life in the UK Test, Australian Citizenship Test, Canadian Citizenship Test, US Citizenship Test, Einbürgerungstest).

Delete from within the app

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account, then tap again to confirm.

This permanently deletes your account and its associated data.

Or request by email

Email nivee12@gmail.com from your account's email address with the subject "Delete my account", and we will delete your account and data.

What is deleted and retained

Deleted: your account identifier (email or phone number) and any associated account data. Study progress stored only on your device is removed when you delete the app. Retained: we may keep minimal records required by law or by the app stores for transaction/subscription history, for the period required, after which they are deleted.
