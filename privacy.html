Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nivee ("we") handles your information in our citizenship-test study apps (Life in the UK Test, Australian Citizenship Test, Canadian Citizenship Test, US Citizenship Test, and Einbürgerungstest), collectively "the apps".

Information we collect

  • Your account identifier (email address or phone number) when you sign up.
  • Your study progress (practice and mock-exam results).
  • If you sign in with Google, the basic profile information you approve.

How we use it

To create and secure your account, save your progress across sessions, provide the study features, show advertising to free users, and process optional subscriptions.

Third-party services

Authentication is handled by Google Firebase; sign-in may use Google; advertising is provided by Google AdMob; subscriptions are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. These providers process data under their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal data.

Advertising

Free users may see ads served by Google AdMob, which may use advertising identifiers. In regions that require it, we request your consent before serving personalized ads. Premium subscribers do not see ads.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account in the app, which removes your account and associated data.

Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy and will revise the date above.
